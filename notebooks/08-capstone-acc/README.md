# DS2002 Capstone: ACC Reality Check

Start with `DS2002 Capstone — ACC Reality Check.pdf`. It describes the project, the two data sources, the questions your team has to answer, the API you will publish, and exactly what to turn in. The appendix explains the basketball terms the project uses, so you do not need to follow the sport to do well.

## The one deadline

The capstone has a single graded submission, due **Friday, December 18, 2026, at 11:59 pm ET**. It includes a recorded team presentation of eight minutes or less, so there are no in-class capstone presentations. Nothing else in this folder is graded or collected. The check-in notebooks below are there to keep your team on pace, and each one builds a piece of the final notebook.

## What else is in this folder

The `ACC Reality Check Starter` template notebook gives your team the section layout for the final notebook. Copy it into your team repository and fill it in there.

`DS2002 PythonAnywhere How-To.pdf`, `DS2002 PythonAnywhere.pptx`, and `pythonanywhere-demo/` come from the deploy lecture. They show how a small FastAPI app goes live on a free PythonAnywhere account, which is the same path your team will take with its own API.
